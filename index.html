What it looks like

The four places

Each stop has a different character: metropolitan, mountain wilderness, waterfall town, and historic riverside settlement.

Changsha

Modern Hunan capital with riverside public spaces, strong food culture and easy metro travel.

Zhangjiajie

Spend four complete days here. The landscape is the reason the itinerary is intentionally unhurried.

Furong Ancient Town

Best enjoyed from afternoon into evening, when the cliffside buildings and waterfall light up.

Fenghuang Ancient Town

A slower final stop: riverside lanes by day, reflections and illuminated architecture at night.

Before you go

Practical checklist

The small setup steps that make travelling in mainland China much smoother.

Payments
Install Alipay + WeChat Pay and add your card before leaving Malaysia.
Trains
Use 12306 or Trip.com. Book popular late-November rail legs once tickets are released.
Stay strategy
Changsha: Wuyi/Furong Square. Zhangjiajie: 3 nights Wulingyuan + 2 nights city.
Weather
Pack layers, waterproof outerwear and good-grip shoes. Mountain conditions can feel much colder than Malaysia.
Passport
Carry the original passport daily; attractions and rail bookings may be linked to it.
Free-ticket rule
Confirm whether “use by 11 Dec” means travel by that date or only redeem/book by then.
